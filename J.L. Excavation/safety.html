Written Safety Program

The program,
not the poster.

A real program in a real binder, in real use on every active site. Not a download from a vendor's template library.

  • Excavation & Trenching — OSHA 1926 Subpart P. Plans submitted on every dig over 5 feet.
  • Confined Space — Permit-required entry program with rescue plan and gas monitoring.
  • Fall Protection — Anchor points, harness inspection, and rescue plan over 6 feet.
  • Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) — Equipment LOTO program and authorized-person training.
  • Hot Work — Permit, fire watch, and gas-monitor protocols on torch / weld work.
  • Hazard Communication — Active SDS binder on every job, accessible to all crew.
  • Crane / Rigging — Per ASME B30 series; signal-person and rigger qualifications documented.
  • Driver Safety — DOT-compliant driver qualification files and HOS logs.
Cadence

What gets done. When.

01

Daily — Pre-shift

  • Job Safety Analysis posted at site office
  • Equipment walk-around and pre-op inspection
  • PPE verification
  • Hazard tour of the work area
  • One-page sign-in by all crew
02

Weekly — Toolbox

  • Topic-based safety talk (15–20 min)
  • Recent incident or near-miss debrief
  • Sign-in by every crew member
  • Written record kept in the site binder
  • Topic rotates through the OSHA matrix
03

Project — Pre-mob

  • Site-specific safety plan submitted
  • Trenching plan per Subpart P
  • Emergency action plan with rescue routes
  • SDS binder localized to the project
  • COIs filed with the GC / owner
Trench Safety

OSHA 1926
Subpart P.
Always.

Every dig over five feet has a written excavation plan, a competent person on site, and approved protective system — trench box, hydraulic shoring, or sloped excavation per soil class.

  • Steel and aluminum trench boxes on the yard
  • Hydraulic shoring for tight-access cuts
  • Ladder access every 25 feet of open trench
  • Daily soil-classification check by competent person
  • Confined-space rescue equipment on every utility job
