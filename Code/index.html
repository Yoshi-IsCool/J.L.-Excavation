About

An established earthwork firm — roots in New York, future in Oklahoma.

Jesse and Kristi Linstruth bring more than 30 years of combined industry experience to every project. After 13 successful years building JL Excavation LLC in upstate New York, they followed God’s call back to Tulsa — where they first met and married — and established JL Earthworks & Design LLC in Mounds, Oklahoma in July 2023.

Today the firm serves Northeastern Oklahoma with comprehensive site preparation, underground utility construction, and GPS modeling services for state, municipal, and commercial clients.
