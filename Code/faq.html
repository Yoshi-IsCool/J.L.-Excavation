We focus on civil earthwork and underground utility construction — site development, grading, water main, sanitary sewer, stormwater, and related work. We take on public, private, and specialty projects of all sizes, from residential homesites to municipal infrastructure.
No, we do not offer surveying services. What we do offer is GPS modeling and field support — building 3D machine-control models from your engineering plans, and providing on-site GPS troubleshooting and field assistance for your crew.
Our primary service area is the Tulsa metro and Northeastern Oklahoma. We’re based in Mounds, OK and routinely work projects across Tulsa, Bixby, Broken Arrow, Sand Springs, Sapulpa, Jenks, Glenpool, and the surrounding region. For larger projects, longer-duration work, or specialty scope, we may travel further — ask us.
Yes. JL Earthworks & Design LLC is fully insured and bonded, with USDOT #2039872. We carry general liability, workers’ comp, and project-specific bonding as needed.
Our team is actively training toward full OSHA certification. We maintain a written safety program, hold weekly safety meetings, and require ongoing safety education across every level of the organization.
