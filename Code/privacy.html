Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026. This policy describes how JL Earthworks & Design LLC handles information collected through this website.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a form on this website — including the contact form, project inquiry, or employment inquiry — we collect the information you voluntarily provide. This typically includes your name, email address, phone number, company name, contact preferences, and the contents of your message.

This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics for behavioral profiling. We may collect anonymous server log data (IP address, browser type, referring page, timestamp) for security and basic site-operation purposes. This data is not used to identify individual visitors.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information you submit through this website is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and continue the conversation about a potential project or position.
  • Communicate with you about ongoing work, schedules, or related business matters.
  • Maintain business records as required by applicable law (tax, accounting, project documentation).

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may share information when required by law, subpoena, or other legal process; when necessary to protect our legal rights; or with trusted service providers (e.g., email hosting) under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

4. Data Security

We take commercially reasonable steps to protect submitted information using industry-standard practices. However, no transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. If you intend to share sensitive information, we recommend calling our office at (918) 938-0608 rather than submitting it through this website.

5. Data Retention

We retain submitted information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected and to satisfy legal, accounting, or business documentation requirements. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at info@jlearthworks.com. We will comply within a reasonable timeframe except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

Depending on the jurisdiction in which you reside, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address above.

7. Cookies

This website does not set tracking cookies. Your browser may store standard session information for security purposes only. If we add functionality requiring cookies in the future, this policy will be updated and notice will be provided.

8. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it.

9. External Links

This website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such third-party sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before submitting any information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the website after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
