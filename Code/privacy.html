Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025. This is a plain-English summary of how JL Earthworks & Design LLC handles information collected through this website.

What we collect

If you submit a form on this site (contact form, project inquiry, employment inquiry, etc.), we collect the information you provide — typically name, contact info, company, and message details. We don’t use cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, follow up about your project, and continue the conversation with you. We don’t sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

How we store it

Form submissions are sent to our internal email and stored in our business systems for project records. Standard business retention applies — we keep records as long as they’re useful for the project relationship and our legal/tax obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing info@jlearthworks.com. We’ll comply within a reasonable timeframe except where business records or legal obligations require us to retain.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information — but no website is 100% secure. If you’re sending us sensitive information, consider a phone call or meeting instead.

Changes

If we update this policy materially, we’ll change the date at the top. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions

Email us at info@jlearthworks.com or call (918) 771-1710.
