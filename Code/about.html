About Us

A measured expansion. A consistent standard.

JL Earthworks & Design LLC represents the next chapter of an established earthwork business with deep industry experience. The predecessor company, JL Excavation LLC, was founded in 2005 and incorporated in 2013 in northern New York, where it earned a strong reputation for safe, dependable earthwork and utility services — including municipal water and sewer infrastructure, commercial site development, and large-scale excavation.

In 2023, the business was re-established in Oklahoma as JL Earthworks & Design LLC, bringing together heavy construction experience and the innovation of in-house GPS machine-control modeling. The firm provides comprehensive site preparation and utility construction services for commercial and municipal projects — managing all phases of earthwork including clearing, grading, excavation, and trenching.

With a focus on long-term performance and reliability, JL Earthworks & Design installs and connects critical utility systems such as water mains, sanitary sewer lines, and stormwater drainage, all in accordance with applicable engineering standards and local regulations.

Leadership

Meet the owners.

President · Operations

Jesse Linstruth

Jesse holds an A.A.S. degree in Math and Science from Jefferson Community College and brings more than 35 years of experience in the earthwork and utility construction industry. Throughout his career, he has served as an Operator, Foreman, Superintendent, and Project Manager.

As owner of JL Earthworks & Design LLC, he oversees all aspects of operations and brings extensive experience in commercial site development, design-build construction, utility installation, GPS-guided grading and layout, estimating, scheduling, and comprehensive project management.

Vice President · Business Operations

Kristi Linstruth

Kristi earned her B.S.Ed. and M.S. in Mathematics from Pittsburg State University and spent eight years teaching mathematics at Jefferson Community College. She later transitioned into the construction industry, bringing a strong foundation in analytical thinking, problem-solving, and organizational leadership.

Kristi plays an integral role in the company’s day-to-day operations, overseeing key administrative and financial functions. Her expertise in strategic planning, budgeting, operational coordination, and business management supports efficient processes and sustained operational performance.
