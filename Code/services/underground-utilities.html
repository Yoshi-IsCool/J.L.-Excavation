Equipment we run

Trench-ready iron.

Excavators with Trench Shoring

Vacuum Trucks

For utility daylighting near existing lines — no blade contact with live gas, water, or telecom.

Compactors

Trench-rammers, plate compactors, and walk-behind rollers for lift-by-lift trench backfill.

Pipe Layers

Side-boom pipe layers and excavator slings for safe handling of large-diameter pipe segments.

Skid Steers

Trench Safety

Steel trench boxes, hydraulic shoring, and confined-space rescue equipment on every active dig.

How a project runs

One-call, dig, document, restore.

Locate & daylight

811 ticket, vacuum-truck daylighting on all crossings before metal touches dirt.

Trench safely

Steel trench boxes or hydraulic shoring per OSHA 1926 Subpart P, written competent-person plan.

Lay & backfill

Bed to spec, lay pipe, joint per manufacturer, lift-by-lift compaction with density testing.

Restore & document

Surface restoration, as-built redlines (GPS-located if specified), turnover package.
