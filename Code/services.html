How a project runs

Four steps. No surprises.

A repeatable process every JL project follows — whether it's a $25K residential foundation or a $900K municipal sewer rehab.

Walk the site

Jesse or a foreman walks the property with you. We confirm scope, surface conflicts, and access.

Quote in writing

Detailed line-item proposal, schedule, mobilization timing, and unit-rate change-order language.

Mobilize & build

Equipment hits the yard, daily JSA, weekly toolbox talk, owner reachable on every shift.

Document & restore

Compaction reports, as-built redlines, surface restoration, final walk. Then we get out of your way.
