Equipment we run

The iron for old pipe and new.

Long-Reach Excavators

For deep utility cuts and large-diameter sewer mains where the rim is more than a stick-length away.

Bypass Pumps

Diesel bypass pumping to keep flow moving while we cut and replace active sanitary or storm.

Vacuum Trucks

Hydro-excavation around live laterals, gas, and water — no blade contact with the unknowns.

Pressure-Test Rigs

Hydrostatic and air testing on water mains and sanitary tie-ins per AWWA / agency spec.

CCTV Coordination

Pre and post-install camera inspection coordinated with the agency's preferred vendor.

Confined-Space Rescue

Tripod, harness, retrieval, and gas monitoring for every manhole and vault entry. Two-person standby always.

How a project runs

From locate to as-built.

Pre-construction

Locate, daylight, bypass-plan, traffic plan, public-notice coordination.

Cut & remove

Saw-cut, excavate to crown, remove old pipe, document conditions encountered.

Install & test

Bed, lay, joint, pressure/leak test, manhole set. Inspector witness on every test.

Restore & turn over

Backfill compaction, surface restoration, as-builts, CCTV final, agency turnover package.
