About

An established earthwork firm — roots in New York, future in Oklahoma.

Jesse and Kristi Linstruth bring more than 35 years of combined earthwork and operations experience to every engagement. After thirteen years building JL Excavation LLC in upstate New York, the principals relocated to the Tulsa area and established JL Earthworks & Design LLC in Oklahoma in July 2023.

Today the firm serves Northeastern Oklahoma with comprehensive site preparation, underground utility construction, and GPS modeling services for state, municipal, and commercial clients.
