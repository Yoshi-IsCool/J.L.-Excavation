Technology

Technology that drives us.

Trimble and Topcon machine-control platforms — bridging the gap between digital design and physical execution. Maximum control, minimum waste, and tolerances as tight as 2 mm on the jobsite.

Trimble

Precise data, automation, and connected workflows.

Trimble solutions enable contractors to leverage precise data, automation, teamwork, and connectivity — fostering reliable project plans and enhancing operational efficiencies both on-site and in manufacturing facilities.

Maximum control. Minimum cost.

Solutions that provide maximum control and efficiency, while also offering a competitive advantage. This empowers us to make profitable decisions, minimize costs and waste, and surpass our clients’ expectations.

2 mm tolerances.

On the jobsite, layout points can be completed five times faster — and a single individual can accurately pinpoint locations from the 3D model with tolerances as tight as 2 mm.

Real-time, accurate data.

3D modeling solutions allow our teams to access real-time, accurate data precisely when needed — all while maintaining a comprehensive understanding of the project’s context and objectives.

Cloud-powered shared space.

Unify your workforce, data, machinery, and processes within a cloud-powered shared space. This enables you to access real-time, practical data and provide your teams, partners, or customers with the necessary resources precisely when they require them.

Topcon

Versatile, high-precision machine control.

By merging the productivity of GNSS positioning with its distinctive zone laser transmitter, Topcon has developed a versatile, high-precision system capable of simultaneously controlling multiple machines.

Minimized overhead investment.

Minimized investment in costly computer systems that demand additional overhead for operation and maintenance.

No intermediaries.

Save time without intermediaries — there’s nothing between sending the plans and breaking ground. A hands-off approach means you won’t have to deal with complex, confusing CAD files or CAD manipulation.

Finished-ground imagery.

Finished-ground imagery so you can precisely envision what your site will look like — before a single bucket of dirt is moved.

Any machine, any size.

Irrespective of your equipment type, Topcon offers a machine control solution designed to enhance efficiency, precision, and substantially minimize setup and downtime.

Applications

Where machine control delivers.

Paving

Leveraging machine-control models for automated steering and planning improves precision in paving procedures — with smoother surfaces and tighter tolerances.

Dozing

Initiating machine-control modeling enhances site safety and enables precise dozing, grading, and scraping — with less time spent setting grade stakes and more time moving dirt.

Excavating

Machine-control modeling services assist excavators in maintaining accurate positional communication for consistent digging precision — especially valuable on utility, foundation, and rock work.
