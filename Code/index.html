What we stand on

Four values, in this order.

Safety sits above money on this jobsite. Integrity is what gets us hired again. Relationships are why we still know the operators we worked with twenty years ago. And family is who's signing the checks.

Safety

Sits above money. Every crew member OSHA-certified. Written program, weekly toolbox talks, daily JSAs on every active site.

Integrity

We do what we say we'll do. The same operator who priced the job runs the machine on it. The owner is reachable on every shift.

Relationships

Nobody is a number. The same crew that finished the last job is the one bidding the next one. Returning clients account for most of our work.

Family

Husband, wife, kids on the team. Decisions made at the kitchen table. Two decades in, we still answer the phone ourselves.

Featured Project · Watertown, NY · 2023

The 500-Block of
Washington Street.

A $910K ARPA-funded municipal sewer replacement — taking out original 1840s stone-and-wood-lined sanitary and storm sewers in the heart of downtown Watertown, plus a new sewer line on Keyes Avenue.

  • Replacement of 19th-century stone-box sewer infrastructure
  • Excavation around live gas, water, and telecom — phased, daylit, documented
  • Full traffic control: barricades, signage, on-site flaggers, one lane open throughout
  • One of eight ARPA-funded projects in Watertown's busiest construction season in decades
