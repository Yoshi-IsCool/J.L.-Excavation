Owner / Operator
The Owner

Jesse Linstruth.
The owner is on the job.

Jesse founded JL Excavation in 2005 after a decade in the field. He runs the company from the cab as much as from the office — when a project hits a problem, the owner is the one walking the trench.

Jesse holds an associate's degree from Jefferson Community College and has been operating heavy iron since 1995. He's known on jobsites for a single rule:

If the dirt isn't right, nothing built on top of it will be either.

Milestones

The work it took to get here.

Twenty years of self-performed earthwork, told the way it actually happened.

2005

JL Excavation begins

Jesse takes on residential excavation and small site-work jobs with one machine and a pickup. The phone rings, the truck rolls.

2010

Active interstate / intrastate carrier authority

USDOT operating authority filed with FMCSA. The company starts hauling its own material instead of subbing it out.

2013

Formal LLC organization

JL Excavation LLC files with state authorities, formalizing what had been a sole-prop operation. Bond capacity, lines of credit, and bidder-list registrations follow.

2015

First public-works contract awards

The company moves into municipal sewer, water, and site-prep contracts under prevailing-wage frameworks.

2018

Multi-crew operation

Crew expands to support concurrent commercial site-development and municipal underground-utility work. Written safety program formalized.

2020

SBA-backed financing during pandemic

The whole crew stays on through the downturn. Nobody gets sent home.

2021

Multi-phase college campus civil contract

Active site-prep work on entryway and infrastructure improvements for a community college green-campus expansion.

2023

$900K+ municipal sewer rehabilitation

Awarded a flagship public-works execution — replacing 1840s-era stone-box sanitary and storm sewers across multiple city blocks.

Today

Multi-crew, multi-discipline

Active municipal, commercial, and residential project pipelines. Same operator on the seat. Same name on the truck.

How we work

Three rules. No exceptions.

Self-performed earthwork.

We do not subcontract the dig. The same JL operator who priced the job runs the machine on it. End-to-end accountability for every cubic yard.

Safety isn't a poster.

Every crew member is OSHA-certified. We run weekly toolbox talks, document trenching plans, and stop work when conditions change.

Owner on the job.

Jesse is reachable on every active project. Not via a project coordinator — directly. That's why we don't scale past what we can stand behind.
