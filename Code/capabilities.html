Equipment

Modern iron, well-maintained, GPS-ready.

Our fleet is sized for the work we do — excavators, dozers, skid steers, and dump trucks ranging from mini to mid-size machines. Equipment is regularly serviced, GPS-equipped where applicable, and ready to deploy.
