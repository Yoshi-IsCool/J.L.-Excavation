Home / About Our Story

Two states.
Twenty years.
One family.

Jesse and Kristi Linstruth started JL Excavation in northern New York in 2005 with one machine and a phone. In 2024, the family brought it to Oklahoma — and rebuilt it as JL Earthworks & Design, with the same hands on the iron and a tech-forward modeling arm under the same roof.

Why we exist

JL Earthworks & Design LLC is the next chapter of a family business with deep roots. Founded in 2005 and incorporated in 2013, our company built a reputation across northern New York for safe, dependable earthwork and utility work — including municipal sewer and water projects, commercial site preparation, and residential earthworks. In 2024, we brought that same family-owned commitment to Oklahoma. Today, we serve the south Tulsa metro from our Mounds headquarters with an expanded scope that combines our hard-earned dirt-work expertise with full-service GPS model design and machine-control modeling.

Owner / Operator
Jesse Linstruth · Owner

Thirty years on the seat.
Same standard.

Jesse founded JL Excavation in 2005 after a decade in the field. He runs the company from the cab as much as from the office — when a project hits a problem, the owner is the one walking the trench.

Jesse holds an associate's degree from Jefferson Community College in Watertown, NY, and has been operating heavy iron since the mid-1990s. On jobsites he's known for a single rule:

Two chapters

Northern New York → Mounds, Oklahoma.

A timeline of how the business grew, what it built, and how it ended up in Creek County.

2005

JL Excavation begins

Jesse takes on residential excavation and small site-work jobs in Chaumont, NY with one machine and a pickup. The phone rings, the truck rolls.

2010

USDOT carrier authority

USDOT #2039872 filed with FMCSA — interstate and intrastate authority. The company starts hauling its own material instead of subbing it out.

2013

Formal LLC organization

JL Excavation LLC files with state authorities, formalizing what had been a sole-prop operation. Bond capacity, banking relationships, and bidder-list registrations follow.

2015

First public-works contract awards

The company moves into municipal sewer, water, and site-prep contracts under prevailing-wage frameworks across Jefferson County.

2018

Multi-crew operation

Crew expands to support concurrent commercial site-development and municipal underground-utility work. Written safety program formalized.

2020

SBA-backed financing during pandemic

Awarded a PPP loan from Watertown Savings Bank. The whole crew of 11 stays on through the downturn. Nobody gets sent home.

2023

$910K Washington Street ARPA project

The City of Watertown selects JL Excavation as the contractor for the 500-block Washington Street sewer rehabilitation — replacing original 1840s stone-and-wood-lined sewers, plus a new sewer line on Keyes Avenue.

2024

Move to Mounds, OK · Rebrand

The family relocates to Oklahoma. The company is reorganized and renamed JL Earthworks & Design LLC at 18955 S 84th W Ave, Mounds. The "& Design" reflects the new GPS model design business arm.

Today

Two arms under one roof

Earthwork and utility work across the south Tulsa metro. GPS model design serving contractors nationwide. Same family. Same standard.

How we work

Three rules. No exceptions.

Self-performed earthwork.

We do not subcontract the dig. The same JL operator who priced the job runs the machine on it. End-to-end accountability for every cubic yard.

Safety isn't a poster.

Every crew member is OSHA-certified. We run weekly toolbox talks, document trenching plans, and stop work when conditions change — even when it costs us a day.

Owner on the job.

Jesse is reachable on every active project. Not via a project coordinator — directly. That's why we don't scale past what we can stand behind.
