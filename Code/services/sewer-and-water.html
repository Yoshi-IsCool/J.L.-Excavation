Scope

Municipal & commercial sewer/water.

JL Earthworks & Design installs and connects critical utility systems — water mains, sanitary sewer lines, and stormwater drainage — for state, municipal, and commercial customers. Everything goes in to engineering spec, with documented inspection and compaction.

  • Water main (PVC, DI, HDPE)
  • Water services & taps
  • Sanitary sewer (gravity & force main)
  • Lift stations (sitework)
  • Sewer manholes & cleanouts
  • Storm drains & structures
  • Catch basins & inlets
  • Detention & retention basins (sitework)
  • Treatment plant earthwork & piping
