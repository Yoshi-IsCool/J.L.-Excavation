Equipment we run

The iron on this work.

Excavators

Multiple sizes from compact to full-size hydraulic excavators for trenching, mass dig, and finish work.

Bulldozers

Crawler dozers for cut/fill balance, rough grade, and stockpile push. GPS machine control where called for.

Off-Road Haulers

Articulated and rigid haulers for moving spoil and import material across the jobsite.

Skid Steers

Detail grade, finish work, and small site access. Bucket and forks for material handling.

Vibratory Rollers

Smooth-drum and pad-foot compactors for subbase, structural fill, and parking lot prep.

GPS Machine Control

Topcon / Trimble grade control where the spec calls for it — survey-quality grade without staking.

How a project runs

Four steps. Documented at each.

Walk the site

Owner-led walk-through. Confirm access, scope, conflicts, and surface elevations.

Earthwork plan

Cut/fill balance, haul-route plan, erosion control, SWPPP coordination — all in writing.

Mobilize & dig

Daily JSA, weekly toolbox talk, machine control set, ongoing compaction testing.

Final grade & docs

Compaction reports, as-built grades, surface restoration, final walk and turnover.

Site Development FAQ

What clients ask before we mobilize.

Depends on density, but a typical 1–3 acre lot with moderate brush and a few trees is a 2–5 day clear. Heavy timber or stumps push that. We size the equipment to the work, not the other way.
We install and maintain perimeter erosion control — silt fence, inlet protection, stabilized construction entrances — and coordinate with the project's SWPPP requirements. Permit pulling is owner/engineer; we execute to the plan.
Yes. Tight-access work is part of the trade — compact equipment, hand work where needed, and shoring on any cut that risks an adjacent footing. We've worked active campuses, occupied homes, and live commercial sites.
Per spec — typically a third-party testing firm runs density tests at lift-rate intervals; we hold each lift until it passes. We provide the test reports as part of the final close-out package.
