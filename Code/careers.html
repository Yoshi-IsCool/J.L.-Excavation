Why JL Earthworks & Design

A culture that respects the work and the people who do it.

We believe strong relationships build strong teams. By treating every employee, client, and partner with respect and professionalism, we foster a culture where people are valued and supported to do their best work.

Safety First

Written safety program maintained on every site. Weekly safety meetings. Stop-work authority extended to every team member. Our team is actively training toward full OSHA certification.

Engaged Leadership

Principals are in the field. Decisions are made with direct project visibility — not from a distant office. Operators have a real voice in how the work gets done.

Modern Tools

Trimble® and Topcon® machine-control systems on the iron that runs them. Operators trained on current technology — not on equipment from twenty years ago.

Roles We Hire

Skilled trades welcome.

We’re typically interested in candidates with experience in one or more of the following. Don’t see your exact role? Reach out anyway — we hear about new positions before they’re posted.

Equipment Operators

Excavator, dozer, skid steer, loader, and dump truck operators with documented experience in earthwork, utility installation, and site development.

Foremen & Superintendents

Field leaders with the experience to run a crew, read plans, manage subs, document inspections, and keep a project moving on schedule and on spec.

Pipe-layers & Laborers

Underground utility installers with experience bedding pipe, setting structures, and working safely in and around shored trenches.

GPS Technicians

Field technicians familiar with Trimble® or Topcon® machine-control setup, calibration, troubleshooting, and as-built capture.

What We Look For

Qualities that matter more than a resume.

Integrity

You do what you say you’ll do. You speak up when something is off. You respect the crew, the client, and the work.

Craftsmanship

You take pride in the finished product. You care about doing the work right, not just done.

Ownership

You take responsibility for your scope. You learn from mistakes. You make the crew better.

How to Apply

Three steps.

Submit an Inquiry

Use our contact form and select Employment Opportunities as your inquiry type. Tell us your role, experience, and what you’re looking for.

We’ll Follow Up

If your background looks like a fit, we’ll reach out within one business day to schedule a conversation.

Conversation & Site Visit

For technical roles, expect to walk a jobsite or run a piece of equipment. We hire on what you can do, not just what you can say.
