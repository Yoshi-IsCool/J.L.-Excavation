Equipment we run

Tight-access iron.

Compact Excavators

3- to 8-ton excavators for tight-lot foundations, basement digs, and footer trenches.

Skid Steers

Detail backfill, slab prep, and material handling around the foundation perimeter.

Plate Compactors

Walk-behind plates and rammers for sub-slab gravel and lift-by-lift trench backfill.

Backhoes

For trench-and-lay scopes, footer drains, and integrated dig-load operations.

Laser Levels

Rotating laser and grade rod for footer-bottom elevations and slab-prep finish.

Trench Safety

Aluminum trench shields for deep footer cuts; sloped excavation otherwise per Subpart P.

How a project runs

From form-board to backfill.

Layout & locate

Foundation layout from staked corners or laid-out form-boards; 811 ticket on every site.

Excavate to spec

Footer or basement to engineer's depth, bottom inspected and approved before forming.

Slab prep

Sub-slab gravel, vapor barrier coordination, plumbing rough-in coordination, compaction.

Backfill & restore

Backfill after waterproofing approval, compaction documented, perimeter restored.
