Privacy Policy.

How JL Excavation LLC handles personal information collected through this website.

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you submit our contact, RFQ, application, or footer form, we collect: name, company, phone number, email, project type, scope description, optional uploaded files (drawings/specs/photos/resume), bid closing date, and your stated source of referral. We also collect basic web analytics — page views, referrer, device type, and approximate region — through a privacy-friendly analytics provider.

How we use it

Personal information is used solely to respond to your inquiry, deliver a quote, evaluate a job application, or fulfill a public-bid response. We don't sell information. We don't share it with third parties beyond our internal team and the surety/insurance broker when a project requires it.

How long we keep it

Active inquiries: retained while the project is open and for a reasonable record-keeping period after. Job applications: retained for one year unless requested otherwise. Web analytics: aggregated, no personally identifying data retained beyond 12 months.

Cookies

This site uses essential cookies for form integrity and a privacy-friendly analytics cookie. We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or third-party tracking.

Your choices

You can request deletion of any personal data you've submitted by emailing info@jlexcavationllc.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Form submissions and uploads are transmitted over HTTPS. Files are stored in access-controlled cloud storage and reviewed only by JL Excavation staff working on your project.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the business evolves. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent change.
