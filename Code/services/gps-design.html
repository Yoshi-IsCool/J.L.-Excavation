GPS Modeling & Field Support

Models engineered to maximize equipment productivity.

3D machine-control models for Trimble® and Topcon® platforms, plus on-site GPS field support. Surveying services are not offered.

Important: Surveying services are not offered.

JL Earthworks & Design provides GPS modeling (3D machine-control models built from your engineering plans) and on-site GPS field support. The firm is not a licensed survey provider and does not perform survey services.

How We Help

3D models + on-site GPS support.

We take your engineering plans and build 3D machine-control models that load directly into Trimble and Topcon equipment — ready for your dozer, grader, excavator, or paver. Faster layout, fewer stakes, tighter tolerances.

If your crew runs into GPS issues on a jobsite, we can help. On-site field support for setup, calibration, troubleshooting, and as-built capture is available.

Send us an inquiry and we can discuss your needs, budget, and go over various options.

What You Get

Process & deliverables.

1. Plans In

Send us your engineering plans (PDF, DWG, or similar). We’ll review and confirm the scope, deliverables, and timeline.

2. Model Built

We build a 3D machine-control model from the plans — tied to your project’s control points — ready for Trimble or Topcon equipment.

3. Model In Iron

The model goes into your machines. We provide setup support and stay available for questions during the job.

4. On-Site Support

If your crew runs into issues in the field, we can come out for hands-on troubleshooting, calibration, or as-built capture.

5. QC & As-Builts

Quality control checks against the design model and digital as-built deliverables — ready for record submission.

6. Revisions

Plans change. We’re ready to update the model and get the revisions back into your iron with minimal downtime.

Technology Platforms

Trimble® & Topcon®.

Both are proven, modern machine-control platforms. We work with both.

Trimble

Layout points completed five times faster. A single individual can pinpoint locations from the 3D model with tolerances as tight as 2 mm. Cloud-powered shared workspace, real-time data, and unified workflows.
