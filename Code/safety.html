Safety

More than a policy on the wall.

At JL Earthworks & Design, safety is an active, enforced part of daily operations — not a binder gathering dust.

At JL Earthworks & Design, safety is an active, enforced part of daily operations. Our safety program is fully documented, maintained on every active project site, and implemented consistently by field teams.

From site-specific procedures and inspections to ongoing training and compliance tracking, our program is designed for real-world execution. This commitment supports accountability, consistency, and a safer working environment on every project we perform.

Program Pillars

Six pillars of our safety program.

Site-Specific Procedures

Every active jobsite has its own safety plan, hazard analysis, and emergency response procedures — documented and reviewed before work starts.

Weekly Safety Meetings

Weekly toolbox talks led by site leadership — covering recent incidents in the industry, jobsite-specific hazards, and reminders on critical procedures.

Ongoing OSHA Training

Our team is actively training toward full OSHA certification. We require continuing safety education across every level of the organization — from operators to ownership.

Compliance Tracking

Documented compliance with OSHA standards, federal and state regulations, and contract-specific safety requirements — tracked and reported.

Daily Inspections

Equipment inspections, fall protection checks, excavation/trench inspections, and PPE verification before work begins each day.

Active & Enforced

Safety standards are enforced consistently across all crews and projects. Stop-work authority is extended to every team member.

Built-In, Not Bolted On

Safety is part of how we run the business.

We’re committed to ensuring the safety of our team members — not because it’s required, but because it’s the foundation of a healthy company and healthy families.

Our leadership is in the field. Our weekly meetings aren’t a formality — they’re a conversation. And our safety program is regularly evaluated and updated based on what we actually see happen on jobsites.

  • Written safety program maintained on every active site
  • Weekly safety meetings with documented sign-offs
  • Crew actively training toward full OSHA certification
  • Daily equipment and excavation inspections
  • Documented compliance tracking on every project
  • Stop-work authority given to every team member

Safety expectations that match yours.

Tell us about your project and any contractor-specific safety requirements. We’ll meet them.
