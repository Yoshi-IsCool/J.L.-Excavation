The pitch

Stakes-free
earthwork.

A 3D site model is the digital twin of the finished job. Your operator sees grade on a screen, the bucket runs to the model, and the dirt goes where the engineer drew it — no surveyor pounding hubs every shift, no flag-and-tape, no “is this right?”

JL Earthworks builds those models for our own jobs and for other contractors who need the design work done by someone who understands how it actually runs. Jesse spent 20+ years on the seat before he ever sat down at a screen — so the model handles the constructability that office-only modelers miss.

  • Existing-conditions surfaces from survey or LiDAR data
  • Subgrade, subbase, and finished-grade surface models
  • Alignment files (centerlines, offsets, super-elevation)
  • Stake-out point files for survey crews
  • Earthwork takeoffs — cut, fill, balance, and import/export quantities
  • File format conversions across Trimble TBC, Topcon MAGNET, Leica Infinity
  • LandXML / DWG / TP3 / TXL / DXF exports
  • As-built model updates from on-site GPS data
  • Constructability review before iron hits dirt
  • Change-order modeling and re-design support

Why us

An operator
built the model.

Most GPS modeling shops are run by CAD techs. The model looks pretty, but the operator hits something the model doesn't account for and the day stops. We've been on both sides — the seat and the screen — so the constructability is in the model before you ever turn a key.

  • Operator-led modeling. Jesse ran iron for 30 years before this. He builds the model the way he wants it on the screen.
  • Constructability built in. Equipment turning radius, haul-route logic, lift-by-lift compaction depth — all reflected in the model surfaces.
  • Cross-platform delivery. Trimble, Topcon, Leica — your fleet, your format.
  • Fast turn. Most jobs delivered inside 5 business days; rush turn available for active sites.
  • Real numbers. Earthwork takeoffs you can build a bid against, not a guess.

How a model project runs

From plan set to job site.

Receive plans

PDF civil set, DWG, survey data, or whatever the engineer hands you. We work from anything.

Build the model

Existing surface, design surfaces, alignments, structures. Constructability review baked in.

Generate machine files

Trimble TP3, Topcon TN3, Leica iCON — whatever your fleet runs. Plus stake-out points and quantities.

Update as-built

As the job progresses, we re-import GPS as-built data and keep the model current for change orders and final close-out.

What you get

The full package.

Each project delivery includes everything your team needs to execute — not just the surface file.

  • Machine control files — Trimble TP3 / Topcon TN3 / Leica iCON, ready to load
  • Surface models — existing, subgrade, subbase, finished grade
  • Alignment files — centerlines, profiles, super-elevation
  • Stake-out points — control point file for the survey crew
